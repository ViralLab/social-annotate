With so many new people joining Mastodon, here’s a few things to note:

1. know your server admin and what power they have. they can see your email, your ip, and your DMs. they are responsible for selecting which other servers will be prohibited on your server.

2. DMs are never private and always unencrypted , if you mention anyone in a DM they will be able to see the message. if you report a DM the admin will see it.

Here’s a good primer on servers: https://www.intego.com/mac-security-blog/mastodon-safety-how-to-protect-yourself-from-security-and-privacy-risks/
